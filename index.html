Understanding aging.
From cells to tissues.

We study how cellular signals and redox balance shape stem cells, tissue homeostasis and aging, using the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster.

01 / RESEARCH

Our research.

Connecting molecular mechanisms with tissue function and organismal phenotypes.

01

Redox biology & aging

Investigating how redox regulation supports stem cell self-renewal and how cells maintain their function during aging.

TISSUE HOMEOSTASIS
02

JAK/STAT signaling

Understanding signaling pathways that control development and how their dysregulation contributes to cancer.

CELL SIGNALING
03

Nanotoxicity

Using Drosophila as an experimental platform to investigate biological responses to engineered nanomaterials.
